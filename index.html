FitKeep

Offline-first tailoring business management for customers, measurements, orders, and Khata records.

About fitKeep

Tailor App helps tailors and tailoring shops manage their daily workflow directly on their mobile device. All records are stored locally on your device using an on-device SQLite database so you can work completely offline.

Key Features

  • Customer Directory: Store customer profiles, contact numbers, and order history.
  • Detailed Measurements: Record and update custom garment measurements accurately.
  • Order Tracking: Manage stitching orders, delivery dates, and garment statuses.
  • Khata & Payments: Track balances, advance payments, and customer ledger entries.
  • Optional Google Drive Backup: Optionally connect your Google Account to securely back up and restore your SQLite database snapshot to your own private Google Drive app-data folder when switching devices.
