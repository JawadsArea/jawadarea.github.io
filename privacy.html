Privacy Policy for Tailor App

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

Tailor App ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Tailor App handles your local business records and how it interacts with Google User Data when you choose to enable optional cloud backup.

1. Local-First Data Storage

Tailor App is designed to work offline without an account. Customer names, phone numbers, garment measurements, orders, Khata entries, and app settings are stored locally in an on-device SQLite database on your phone. We do not operate external servers that collect, read, or store your business data.

2. Google Account & Google Drive Data Access

Connecting a Google Account is strictly optional. If you choose to enable Google Drive Backup in the app, Tailor App requests access to the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • Basic Account Identity (userinfo.email, userinfo.profile, openid): Used solely to display your connected Google email address and identify your account inside the app's backup settings.
  • Google Drive App Data Folder (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata): Used solely to upload, update, and download your app's backup file inside the hidden, private appDataFolder of your own Google Drive account. Tailor App cannot view, read, modify, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive.

3. How Your Data Is Used and Shared

  • Your backup data is transmitted securely over HTTPS directly between your device and your personal Google Drive account.
  • We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer your Google user data or tailoring records to any third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers.
  • We do not use any Google user data to train AI or machine learning models.
  • Tailor App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Retention, Deletion, and Revoking Access

  • Local Data: You can delete records directly inside the app or remove all local data by uninstalling the app from your device.
  • Cloud Backup Data: Your backup file remains in your private Google Drive app-data folder until you overwrite it with a new backup or delete the app's hidden data through your Google Drive settings (Google Drive > Settings > Manage apps).
  • Revoking Permissions: You can disconnect your Google account inside Tailor App at any time or revoke access via your Google Account Permissions page.
